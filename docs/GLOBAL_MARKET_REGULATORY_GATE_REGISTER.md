## 1. Executive decision

**Current global release status: NO-GO for public token offers, paid token promotion, token sales, real-value payouts, public mining activation, Mainnet launch, or exchange/listing claims.**

The current repository describes V COIN – TON as under development. The tokenomics document is approved for design, not issuance. The reviewed readiness register states that no verified Jetton deployment, wallet-based mining, production database operation, independent security audit, or jurisdiction-specific legal opinion has been established. The mining API is deliberately fail-closed with HTTP 503 until wallet-based mining is implemented and verified.

The global programme may continue with evidence-led project-development communications, technical documentation and carefully controlled B2B research. Such communications must not invite token purchases, imply availability, or describe roadmap features as live. Every country-specific communication remains subject to local legal review.

## 2. Programme continuity and approved direction

The following earlier work is retained; it is not repeated here:

- Brand positioning and core narrative have been drafted.
- The priority brand line is: **“Those Who Missed the Beginning, Can Still Be Part of a New Beginning.”**
- The project is now an independent TON / Jetton (TEP-74) implementation. No BNB-chain contracts, addresses, claims or operating assumptions are to be reused.
- Market work previously prioritised Europe for technical credibility, Bahrain as the first Arab-market legal-screening route, and the UAE for conditional B2B exploration.
- Mainland China, Qatar and Kuwait remain excluded from campaign activity. Saudi Arabia remains on hold for sales solicitation and recruitment-style promotion pending legal review.
- LinkedIn, X and GitHub are the first channels for technical progress and documentation. Telegram promotion follows only after the bot/Mini App and wallet flow are verified. Specialist media, paid advertising, influencers and ambassadors remain gated by evidence and legal clearance.

These are programme decisions, not findings that any market has approved VCOIN.

### Market-screening notes

1. “B2B exploration” means private, non-solicitation discussions about technology, research or potential partnerships. It does not authorise custody, exchange, brokerage, investment advice, token distribution or payment services.
2. A public website, social post, influencer video, referral link, airdrop, event or educational article can still be regulated marketing depending on the jurisdiction and its content.
3. Geofencing and distribution controls must be implemented where required; a disclaimer alone does not cure an otherwise prohibited activity.
4. This register is a screening tool, not a substitute for a written opinion from counsel qualified in each target jurisdiction.

## 4. Public claims: permitted now vs. prohibited pending evidence

### Permitted now, with “under development” prominent

- “V COIN – TON is an independent project under development on The Open Network (TON).”
- “The project has documented a proposed TON Jetton design and a tokenomics model.”
- “The design specifies a planned initial supply of 10,000,000,000 VCOIN, six decimal places and no additional minting; this is a design specification, not proof of issuance.”
- “Wallet-based mining, Jetton deployment, payouts and production operations remain subject to implementation, testing and review.”
- “The project is developing security, accounting and transparency controls.”

### Prohibited until supported by verifiable evidence and legal clearance

- “VCOIN is live, issued, deployed or available on TON Mainnet.”
- “Mining/extraction is live” or “users can withdraw rewards.”
- “The Jetton contract is audited, fully secure or regulator-approved.”
- “VCOIN is listed, liquid, accepted worldwide or supported by exchanges.”
- Any guaranteed price, profit, return, appreciation, liquidity, adoption or fixed market value.
- Any comparison designed to imply that VCOIN is equivalent or superior to Bitcoin or another unrelated asset.
- Any statement that a regulator, government, TON, Telegram, wallet provider, exchange or partner endorses VCOIN without verifiable written permission.
- Any presentation of a modelled reference-price series or illustrative candles as actual market prices or trading history.

## 5. Founder privacy and accountability

A public-facing pseudonym may be used only where lawful and where it does not mislead users about who is responsible for the project. Confidential public presentation of the founder's personal identity or country of origin does not remove the duty to disclose, through secure and lawful processes, the responsible legal entity, beneficial ownership, controlling persons, authorised signatories, treasury control and required records to regulators, banks, auditors and contractual counterparties.

Before any public offer or regulated activity, publish or make appropriately accessible:
- the responsible legal entity and a working legal contact channel;
- the entity's actual regulatory status in each relevant market;
- the token's rights, limitations, supply design and allocation disclosures;
- treasury custody and approval policy, including who is accountable for controls;
- conflicts of interest, fees, material risks and complaint handling;
- the status of independent audits and unresolved findings.

Do not claim absolute anonymity. Public-chain transactions and balances may be observable and analysed.
