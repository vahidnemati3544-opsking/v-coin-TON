Critical transaction notice

This page never receives or stores wallet keys. It can only ask the connected Tonkeeper-compatible wallet to sign transactions after you press a button.

No automatic transaction is sent. The first transaction deploys the Master and funds its reserve. The five VCOIN allocations are five separate Founder-signed transactions after deployment is confirmed on-chain.

Verify the network, sender, destination, TON amount, and message in your wallet before every signature. Never share a seed phrase, private key, PIN, or recovery phrase.

Step 2 — Confirm Master deployment

Do not issue tokens until the Master is active on TON Mainnet and its on-chain getters match the five registered addresses.

Step 3 — Issue the five fixed allocations

Connect the exact Founder wallet. Each button submits one VCoinMint message to the Master. The Master deploys/credits the corresponding deterministic Jetton Wallet. Do not press again until the previous transaction is confirmed and its balance/state has been checked.

Release checks

  • Initial fixed supply: 2,100,000,000 VCOIN; 6 decimals. Modelled USD reference price: $0.004 per VCOIN (not a market quote).
  • Founder: 420,000,000; Mining reserve: 1,050,000,000.
  • Treasury: 210,000,000; Liquidity/Market: 210,000,000; Ecosystem/Marketing: 210,000,000.
  • After all five credits: total_supply must equal 1.0 × 10^16 base units and mintable must be false.
  • No Master function can claw back, freeze, or confiscate recipient Jetton balances.
  • Predicted Jetton Wallet addresses must be confirmed using the deployed Master's get_wallet_address getter.
