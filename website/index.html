The ecosystem vision

V COIN is being developed as a modular TON-native ecosystem. The components below describe the project's direction; they are not a claim that every feature is already available.

TON-native foundation

Independent architecture designed for The Open Network and its Jetton token standard.

Participation & rewards

A planned mining, activity and progression framework, subject to implementation and testing.

Wallet connectivity

A security-first approach to wallet integration and user-controlled asset interactions.

Transparent accounting

Planned internal records and auditable accounting principles for balances and rewards.

Telegram experience

A planned Telegram-based access layer for ecosystem participation and user interaction.

Digital payments

A long-term direction for digital payment utility, dependent on technical and legal readiness.

Security before deployment
